About This Project

Technology is the use of knowledge and tools to solve problems. Over the last 200 years, inventions in communication and computing have changed how we talk, learn, work and play. This page looks at some of the biggest steps along the way.

Timeline

1800s: Communication at a distance

  • 1844: Samuel Morse sends the first long-distance telegraph message.
  • 1876: Alexander Graham Bell patents the telephone.

1900s: Radio, TV and computers

  • 1920s-1930s: Radio and television reach homes.
  • 1945: ENIAC, one of the first general-purpose electronic computers, is completed.
  • 1947: The transistor is invented, making computers smaller and faster.

1960s-1990s: Computers connect

  • 1969: ARPANET, an early ancestor of the internet, goes online.
  • 1970s-1980s: Personal computers arrive in homes and schools.
  • 1973: The first handheld mobile phone call is made.
  • 1989-1991: Tim Berners-Lee invents the World Wide Web.

2000s to today: Always connected

  • 2007: The iPhone makes smartphones popular.
  • 2010s: Social media, streaming and cloud storage become part of daily life.
  • 2020s: Artificial intelligence tools become widely available.

Impact on Our Lives

  • Communication: Messages that took days now arrive in seconds.
  • Education: Students can learn almost anything online.
  • Work: Many jobs can now be done from anywhere.
  • Challenges: Screen time, privacy and fake information are new problems to manage.
